Lagos, Nigeria

Lagos is my favourite city in Nigeria because it is full of life, culture, business opportunities, and beautiful places. It is the largest city in Nigeria and one of the most populated cities in Africa. Lagos is known for its busy markets, modern buildings, beaches, entertainment industry, and friendly people. Many businesses and industries are located in Lagos, making it the economic centre of the country. Visitors can enjoy places such as Lekki Conservation Centre, Tarkwa Bay Beach, and the famous Lekki-Ikoyi Link Bridge. The city is also known for its music, fashion, food, and nightlife. Lagos combines modern development with rich cultural traditions, making it an exciting place to live, work, and visit. I like Lagos because there is always something interesting to see, learn, or experience.
